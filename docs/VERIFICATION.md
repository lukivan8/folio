## Remaining limits and exceptions

- The application is an unsigned local macOS arm64 build. Public distribution still needs signing, notarization, and a clean-machine installer check.
- Sources are imported snapshots, not live connectors. The independent source-exploration library is local; individual portable documents include their own source/revision bundles, not the library's entire chat history.
- Existing custom artifact source is preserved. Recharts and three isolated-frame legacy color-variable aliases remain for compatibility; some hard-coded internal geometry/palettes in saved custom views remain until revised. There are no Material components or styles in the host. New templates and authoring default to Carbon.
- In-flight AI requests do not automatically resume after application restart. Working artifacts, sent messages, drafts, and retained failed candidates are saved; retry is explicit.
- The supported three-pane editor, Tiptap contentDOM/dragging, and caret-anchored commands are product-specific compositions, detailed in the canonical reference. Carbon primitives provide their controls and behavior.
- A full independent accessibility/security audit, target-hardware performance benchmarks for maximum sources/large documents, and human usability studies remain release work. The full-workspace sandbox flow is interaction-tested; this is not an accessibility conformance certificate.
- Ambiguous dates remain text until accepted or corrected. There is no general locale/timezone conversion engine. Analytical checks are supporting evidence, not independent verification.

Upstream Carbon Sass currently emits deprecation notices with the pinned Sass compiler; builds pass. No source content is rewritten to silence warnings. Production dependency audit excludes development-only tooling advisories.
